- Human camera and smart transitions (engine/app/pipeline/camera.py, applied in compose.plan_for after shots.plan): fill shots (and a pip's fill base) get per-frame crops in Shot.motion — mid-shot subject changes glide (eased 380–750 ms, a 90 ms reaction), changes within 140 ms of a camera cut snap and re-aims further than 0.6 of the crop width cut (CUT_OVER_SHARE, like an editor cutting between people sitting apart), tracked paths follow a critically damped spring; long shots breathe 3–6% (alternating push/pull, pivoting on the eye line); zoom "auto" adds punch-ins (+12%, 180/420/650 ms) on loud or stressed words (8 kHz RMS z-score + cue words, ≥3.5 s apart, ~1 per 7 s, clear of shot edges) and a 1.4 s hook on the first shot; "off" holds still.
