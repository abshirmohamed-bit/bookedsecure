- Human camera and smart transitions (engine/app/pipeline/camera.py, applied in compose.plan_for after shots.plan): fill shots (and a pip's fill base) get per-frame crops in Shot.motion — mid-shot subject changes glide (eased 380–750 ms, a 90 ms reaction), changes within 140 ms of a camera cut snap, tracked paths follow a critically damped spring; long shots breathe 3–6% (alternating push/pull, pivoting on the eye line); zoom "auto" adds punch-ins (+12%, 180/420/650 ms) on loud or stressed words (8 kHz RMS z-score + cue words, ≥3.5 s apart, ~1 per 7 s, clear of shot edges) and a 1.4 s hook on the first shot; "off" holds still. Shots whose composition changes without a camera cut get Shot.transition (an xfade name); compose.join_shots overlaps them by 200 ms (each side trimmed 100 ms longer) so the clip keeps its exact length, while real cuts stay concat. Moving shots render as a fixed crop around the whole move + perspective (eval=frame, flat clip() ramp expressions of `in`, sub-pixel), never per-frame crop size changes: ffmpeg 7.1's scale reinitialises framesync on input size changes and silently truncates the video (Fly runs 7.1.5). The framing payload carries motion (every 3rd frame, clip-relative) and transition; LivePreview and clip-render play them through lib/clip-motion (cropAt, transitionAt, transitionLook).
